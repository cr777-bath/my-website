Welcome

I am a Senior Lecturer (Associate Professor) in Statistics in the Department of Mathematical Sciences at the University of Bath. My research considers the statistical analysis of environmental and financial data, and the development of methodology in extreme value analysis, spatial statistics and nonparametric regression. I am also a member of the executive team of the EPSRC Centre for Doctoral Training in Statistical Applied Mathematics (SAMBA), as well as, Lead of the Environmental Extremes theme and Academic Fellow at the Institute for Mathematical Innovation. Prior to joining the University of Bath in December 2019, I held an AXA Research Fund postdoctoral grant and an EPSRC Doctoral Prize at Lancaster University. I completed my PhD in Statistics and Operational Research at the STOR-i Centre for Doctoral Training at Lancaster Lancaster University from 2013 to 2016.
